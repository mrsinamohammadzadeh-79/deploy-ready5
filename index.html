Our Story

A Legacy of Precision

For nearly two centuries, our atelier has pursued a singular vision — to create timepieces that transcend their function and become heirlooms of enduring beauty. Each generation of master watchmakers has refined our techniques while honoring the traditions that define our craft.

Every timepiece that leaves our workshop carries the dedication of hundreds of skilled hands — a promise of precision that will endure for generations.

— Since 1832
